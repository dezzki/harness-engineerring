This is the first post on this website,  making it a good place to
explain why the site exists and how it runs.

## Why this site exists

I'm a third-year CS student focusing on harness engineering, the systems
that makes AI coding agents reliable. I'm studying it hands-on, and I want to
document that journey here.

The goal is simple: A place where I write about what I'm working on, 
what I'm learning, and what breaks along the way.

## A Clean digital presence  

The idea of staying off-grid and not document what I do along the way seemed a
off putting to me. Not  a massive multi-page web app on day one but enough of
a start to  call this my clean digital hub.

This place is a central hub to navigate through my journey :

1. **About Me** : Who I am and what am I currently up to.
2. **Quick links** : Direct links to my GitHub, Contact info and other Socials.
3. **Projects and Writings** : Direct connection to repositories and latest writings.

## What I'll write about

This space is going to be my public Notebook.
Mostly things I'm learning while building:

- harness engineering for AI coding agents
- notes from my projects
- web development, Linux, and tooling and random 3am experiments
- mistakes and lessons that are worth writing down

Thanks for stopping by.
